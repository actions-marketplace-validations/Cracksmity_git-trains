Read `tools/conductor/tasks/regions-brief.md` first: it is the brief for this task.

Add a `region` block to two existing example skylines. Change nothing else in them: do not touch `buildings`, `name`, `description`, `fill` or any other key (a gate compares the files without `region` to the originals).

1. `examples/skylines/mexico-city.json`: the Valley of Mexico (research section 3). Terrain `mountains` (a basin ringed by volcanic ranges). Houses: the Milpa Alta volcanic-stone house with the deeply recessed door. Trees: the ahuejote (narrow, upright willow of the chinampas), the ahuehuete (Montezuma cypress: thick flared trunk, broad crown with soft hanging tips) and a third tree (the nopal as a tall opuntia, or a jacaranda of the city's avenues). Plants: the nopal as a low plant (opuntia pads) and, if you have a fourth slot, a low milpa (maize) row or a clump of marigold (cempasuchil, with a spot of orange). Landmark: the chinampa plot and canal (15 x 6 ground motif: raised bed, crop strip, blue-gray channel, edge stakes), `place: "village"`.
2. `examples/skylines/medellin.json`: Antioquia, Colombia (research section 4). Terrain `mountains` (steep green slopes and layered Andean ridges). Houses: the coffee-country farmhouse with its shaded front veranda (two thin posts and a railing). Trees: guadua bamboo (three slender canes with dark nodes and arching leaf sprays), the guayacán (yellow trumpet tree in flower: a few thick branches, an open crown with yellow blossom pixels) and a third tree of your choice from the research or a broad shade tree typical of coffee farms. Plants: the arabica coffee shrub (compact oval, paired leaf tiers, a few white blossom or red berry pixels). Landmark: the coffee solar dryer (low arched translucent cover on dark posts over an ochre bed), `place: "village"`.

Check each with `node scripts/region-sheet.js examples/skylines/medellin.json .scratch/med.png 8` and in the scene (see the brief). The gates run `node tools/conductor/gates/region-only.js` (the files, minus `region`, equal the originals), `node tools/conductor/gates/region-art.js` and the tests.
