Read `tools/conductor/tasks/regions-brief.md` first: it is the brief for this task. Also read `docs/ai/skyline.md` (how to draw a skyline: blocks with tops and drawn `rows`) and look at `examples/skylines/mexico-city.json` and `medellin.json` for the style of a finished skyline.

Create two new complete example skylines, each with its city **and** its region, in `examples/skylines/` (the only place you may write):

1. `examples/skylines/guadalajara.json`: Guadalajara, Jalisco, Mexico.
   - City (the `buildings`, `fill: true`): four to six iconic buildings from left to right, exaggerated a little so each is recognizable by its silhouette: the Cathedral with its two tall slender neo-Gothic spires; the Teatro Degollado (neoclassical portico under a triangular pediment, with a low dome behind); the Hospicio Cabañas (a very long, low neoclassical building with a central dome); the Templo Expiatorio (neo-Gothic, a tall central spire flanked by two lower ones); and one or two modern towers of the city. Keep what you want people to see taller than 30 px (the train covers the lower 25 px) and no tall tower at x < 90 (the station sign) or around x 382-402, y 10-30 (the sun).
   - Region (research section 2): terrain `plains` (inland valleys); houses: the adobe village house with its one-pixel eave; trees: the jacaranda in violet bloom, the mesquite, and a third (a native tree from the research, or a fig or a palm of the plazas); plants: the blue agave rosette (this is the field crop: the plant sprite is a rosette, not a tree); landmark: the Tequila volcano, 13 x 7 at least (draw it bigger if it reads better, up to 32 x 28), `place: "far"`, so it rises on the horizon behind the agave fields.
2. `examples/skylines/malmo.json`: Malmö, Skåne, southern Sweden.
   - City (`fill: true`): the Turning Torso (a slender tower whose segments twist: draw the offset stacked blocks with a few `w` windows), Sankt Petri kyrka (brick Gothic church with a tall slender copper spire), Malmöhus castle (low, with round corner towers) and one or two plain modern buildings. Same constraints as above (taller than 30 px, clear of the sign and the sun).
   - Region (research section 6): terrain `sea` (the Öresund horizon off the Skåne coast); houses: the Scanian half-timbered farmhouse with a steep thatched gable (two timber divisions and one diagonal brace) and, if you can keep it recognizable, a second variant (a whitewashed brick or plain farm building); trees: the European beech, the pedunculate oak, the European hornbeam; plants: one low plant of Skåne (rapeseed in yellow bloom, a hay bale, a rose-hip hedge or tall grass); landmark: the traditional Scanian windmill, `place: "village"`.

Both files: `name` in the city's own spelling ("Guadalajara", "Malmö"), a `description` of what is drawn (leave `author` out), and `region.source` listing the sources from the research. Check them with `node scripts/region-sheet.js examples/skylines/guadalajara.json .scratch/gdl.png 8` and the real scene (see the brief; render light and dark, and a single-track render too: `--mode single`, which shows the skyline and the slope).
